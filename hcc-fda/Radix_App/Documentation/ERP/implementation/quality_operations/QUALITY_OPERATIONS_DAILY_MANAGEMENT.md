## 🎯 QUALITY OPERATIONS MISSION

### System Purpose
Provide practical, production-ready tools for daily quality operations management to ensure consistent compliance, effective problem solving, and continuous improvement.

### Operational Objectives
1. **Real-Time Monitoring:** Track quality events as they occur
2. **Rapid Response:** Address issues immediately and effectively
3. **Data-Driven Decisions:** Use metrics to guide actions
4. **Continuous Improvement:** Learn from every event
5. **Regulatory Compliance:** Maintain 100% compliance daily

## 📅 DAILY QUALITY OPERATIONS SCHEDULE

### Daily Routine (Monday-Friday)

**7:00 AM - Start of Day**
- [ ] Review overnight production/lab reports
- [ ] Check email for urgent quality issues
- [ ] Review daily quality dashboard
- [ ] Identify priority items for the day

**7:30 AM - Daily Quality Standup Meeting (15 minutes)**
**Attendees:** QA Manager, Production Supervisor, Lab Supervisor, QC Supervisor

**Agenda:**
1. Safety moment (2 min)
2. Previous day summary (3 min)
   - Batches reviewed/released
   - Deviations reported
   - CAPA updates
   - Key metrics
3. Today's priorities (5 min)
   - Batches to be released today
   - Critical reviews pending
   - Inspections/audits
4. Issues requiring attention (3 min)
5. Resource needs (2 min)

**8:00 AM - Begin Daily Activities**
- [ ] Batch record reviews
- [ ] Deviation investigations
- [ ] CAPA follow-ups
- [ ] Change control reviews
- [ ] Training compliance checks
- [ ] Document reviews/approvals

**12:00 PM - Mid-Day Check**
- [ ] Review morning activities
- [ ] Check for new deviations
- [ ] Monitor production status
- [ ] Address urgent issues

**3:00 PM - Afternoon Quality Review**
- [ ] Final batch reviews
- [ ] Close out investigations
- [ ] Update tracking systems
- [ ] Prepare next day priorities

**5:00 PM - End of Day**
- [ ] Complete daily dashboard update
- [ ] Send daily quality report
- [ ] Handoff to night shift QA (if applicable)
- [ ] Prepare tomorrow's priorities list

## 📝 DEVIATION INVESTIGATION WORKFLOW

### Deviation Identification & Reporting

**Step 1: Identification (Immediate)**
- Employee identifies deviation from procedure/specification
- Stop and secure the area/product
- Notify supervisor immediately

**Step 2: Initial Notification (Within 15 minutes)**
- Supervisor notifies QA
- Complete Deviation Notification Form (quick form)
- Document:
  - What happened
  - When it occurred
  - Who discovered it
  - What product/batch affected
  - Immediate containment actions

**Step 3: Initial Classification (Within 1 hour)**
**QA classifies severity:**

**Critical:** Immediate impact to product quality or safety
- Stop all related activities
- Quarantine all potentially affected product
- Notify management immediately
- Begin investigation within 4 hours

**Major:** Potential impact to product quality
- Assess extent
- Implement containment
- Begin investigation within 24 hours

**Minor:** Unlikely impact to product quality
- Document occurrence
- Begin investigation within 5 days

### Investigation Process

**Phase 1: Initial Investigation (Days 1-5)**

**Day 1 - Investigation Kickoff:**
- [ ] Assign investigator
- [ ] Form investigation team (if needed)
- [ ] Review deviation details
- [ ] Secure evidence
- [ ] Interview personnel
- [ ] Document initial findings

**Investigation Checklist:**
- [ ] What exactly happened? (describe in detail)
- [ ] When did it happen? (exact time/date)
- [ ] Where did it happen? (specific location)
- [ ] Who was involved? (all personnel)
- [ ] How was it discovered? (detection method)
- [ ] What is the extent? (how much product affected)
- [ ] Could it have happened before? (review history)

**Day 2-3 - Root Cause Analysis:**

**Use 5 Whys Technique:**
```
Problem: [State the problem clearly]

Why 1: [First reason]
Why 2: [Deeper reason]
Why 3: [Even deeper]
Why 4: [Getting to root]
Why 5: [Root cause]

Root Cause: [Final determination]
```

**Or Fishbone Diagram:**
```
Categories to Consider:
- People (training, knowledge, attention)
- Process (procedures, design, controls)
- Materials (quality, suitability, handling)
- Equipment (design, maintenance, calibration)
- Environment (temperature, cleanliness, space)
- Measurement (methods, accuracy, calibration)
```

**Day 4 - Impact Assessment:**
- [ ] Product quality impact
- [ ] Other batches affected
- [ ] Systems/processes affected
- [ ] Regulatory impact
- [ ] Customer impact
- [ ] Safety impact

**Day 5 - Investigation Report Draft:**
- [ ] Summary of investigation
- [ ] Root cause identified
- [ ] Impact assessment complete
- [ ] CAPA recommendations
- [ ] QA review

**Phase 2: CAPA Development (Days 6-10)**

**Corrective Actions (Fix the immediate problem):**
1. [Specific corrective action]
   - Owner: [Name]
   - Due date: [Date]
   - Success criteria: [How measured]

2. [Additional corrective action]
   - Owner: [Name]
   - Due date: [Date]
   - Success criteria: [How measured]

**Preventive Actions (Prevent recurrence):**
1. [Specific preventive action]
   - Owner: [Name]
   - Due date: [Date]
   - Success criteria: [How measured]

2. [Additional preventive action]
   - Owner: [Name]
   - Due date: [Date]
   - Success criteria: [How measured]

**Phase 3: Implementation & Verification (Days 11-30)**

- [ ] Implement corrective actions
- [ ] Implement preventive actions
- [ ] Update procedures (if needed)
- [ ] Train personnel (if needed)
- [ ] Verify effectiveness (check that actions work)
- [ ] QA approval of closure

**Phase 4: Closure (Day 30)**

- [ ] All actions complete
- [ ] Effectiveness verified
- [ ] Documentation complete
- [ ] QA final review
- [ ] Management approval
- [ ] Close deviation in system
- [ ] Communicate lessons learned
