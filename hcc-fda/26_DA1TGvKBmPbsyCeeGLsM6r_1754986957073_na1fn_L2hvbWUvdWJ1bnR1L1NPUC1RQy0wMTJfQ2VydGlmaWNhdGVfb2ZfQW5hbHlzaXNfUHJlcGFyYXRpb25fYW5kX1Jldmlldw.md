# Standard Operating Procedure

## SOP-QC-012: Certificate of Analysis Preparation and Review

**Version:** 1.0
**Effective Date:** 2025-12-08
**Category:** Quality Control
**Compliance Areas:** FDA, USDA, cGMP

## 1. Purpose and Scope

This Standard Operating Procedure (SOP) outlines the process for the preparation and review of Certificates of Analysis (CoAs) for all large animal pharmaceutical products manufactured by [Company Name]. The purpose of this SOP is to ensure that all CoAs are accurate, complete, and compliant with internal specifications, regulatory requirements, and industry standards. This SOP applies to all personnel involved in the generation, review, approval, and distribution of CoAs, including Quality Control (QC) laboratory personnel, Quality Assurance (QA) personnel, and production staff involved in data provision.

## 2. Responsibilities

### 2.1 Quality Control (QC) Laboratory Personnel

QC Laboratory Personnel are responsible for:
* Performing all required analytical testing on raw materials, in-process samples, and finished products.
* Accurately recording all test results and raw data.
* Preparing the draft Certificate of Analysis based on approved templates and verified test results.
* Ensuring all calculations are correct and data transcription is accurate.
* Initiating the review process for the CoA.

### 2.2 Quality Assurance (QA) Personnel

QA Personnel are responsible for:
* Reviewing the completed Certificate of Analysis for accuracy, completeness, and compliance with all applicable specifications and regulatory requirements.
* Verifying that all required tests have been performed and that results meet acceptance criteria.
* Ensuring proper documentation and traceability of all data referenced in the CoA.
* Approving the final Certificate of Analysis.
* Managing the distribution of approved CoAs to relevant internal and external stakeholders.

### 2.3 Production Personnel

Production Personnel are responsible for:
* Providing accurate and timely information regarding batch numbers, manufacturing dates, and other relevant production data required for the CoA.
* Collaborating with QC and QA to resolve any discrepancies or issues related to production data.

## 3. Detailed Procedures

### 3.1 Preparation of the Certificate of Analysis

3.1.1 **Data Collection and Verification:**

*   QC Laboratory Personnel shall collect all analytical test results for the specific batch of product requiring a CoA. This includes results for raw materials, in-process checks, and finished product testing.
*   All raw data, chromatograms, spectra, and other supporting documentation must be verified for accuracy and completeness against laboratory records.
*   Ensure all testing was performed using validated methods and calibrated equipment.

3.1.2 **CoA Generation:**

*   Utilize the approved electronic or hardcopy CoA template (Form QC-012-F01).
*   Enter all relevant product information, including product name, batch number, manufacturing date, expiry date, and storage conditions.
*   Accurately transcribe all verified test results into the designated fields on the CoA template. Ensure units of measure are correct and consistent.
*   Include details of the analytical methods used for each test.
*   Calculate and record any derived values (e.g., purity, potency) as per established procedures.
*   Attach any necessary supporting documentation, such as chromatograms or raw data printouts, as required by the template or product specification.

### 3.2 Review of the Certificate of Analysis

3.2.1 **Initial QC Review:**

*   A second QC Laboratory Personnel, not involved in the initial preparation, shall perform an independent review of the draft CoA.
*   Verify all data entries against original laboratory records and raw data.
*   Check all calculations for accuracy.
*   Confirm that all required tests have been performed and that results meet specified acceptance criteria.
*   Ensure the CoA is free from typographical errors and formatting inconsistencies.
*   Sign and date the QC review section upon completion.

3.2.2 **Quality Assurance (QA) Review and Approval:**

*   The draft CoA, along with all supporting documentation, shall be submitted to QA for final review and approval.
*   QA Personnel shall conduct a comprehensive review, focusing on:
    *   **Compliance:** Verification against internal specifications, pharmacopoeial requirements (e.g., USP, EP), and relevant regulatory guidelines (FDA, USDA, cGMP).
    *   **Completeness:** Ensuring all required information is present and accurately represented.
    *   **Accuracy:** Cross-referencing all data with original laboratory records and batch production records.
    *   **Traceability:** Confirming that all data can be traced back to its source.
    *   **Integrity:** Ensuring data integrity throughout the process.
*   Any discrepancies or deviations identified during the QA review must be immediately communicated to the QC Laboratory Personnel for resolution. The CoA shall not be approved until all issues are satisfactorily addressed and documented.
*   Upon satisfactory review, QA Personnel shall sign and date the approval section of the CoA, indicating final authorization.

### 3.3 Distribution of Approved CoA

*   Once approved by QA, the final CoA shall be distributed to relevant internal departments (e.g., Sales, Shipping, Regulatory Affairs) and external customers as per established distribution lists.
*   Maintain a controlled copy of all approved CoAs in a secure, designated archive.

## 4. Documentation Requirements

All documentation related to the preparation and review of CoAs must be maintained in accordance with Good Documentation Practices (GDP) and company record retention policies. This includes, but is not limited to:
*   Raw analytical data and laboratory notebooks.
*   Instrument printouts and electronic data files.
*   Completed CoA templates (draft and final versions).
*   Review and approval checklists.
*   Any deviation reports or investigations related to CoA data.
*   Distribution records of approved CoAs.

## 5. Training Requirements

All personnel involved in the preparation, review, and approval of Certificates of Analysis must receive initial and ongoing training on this SOP. Training shall cover:
*   The contents of this SOP.
*   Good Documentation Practices (GDP).
*   Relevant analytical methods and testing procedures.
*   Data integrity principles.
*   Regulatory requirements pertaining to CoAs.
*   Use of CoA templates and associated software/systems.
*   Training records must be maintained by the Human Resources department and be readily available for inspection.

## 6. Quality Control Measures

*   **Method Validation:** All analytical methods used for testing products must be validated according to ICH guidelines and internal validation protocols.
*   **Equipment Calibration and Maintenance:** All laboratory equipment used for testing must be regularly calibrated and maintained according to established schedules and procedures.
*   **Reference Standards and Reagents:** Use only qualified reference standards and reagents with valid Certificates of Analysis.
*   **Proficiency Testing:** Participate in external proficiency testing programs where available and relevant to verify laboratory competence.
*   **Internal Audits:** Conduct periodic internal audits of the CoA preparation and review process to ensure compliance with this SOP and identify areas for improvement.

## 7. Safety Considerations

Personnel involved in the preparation of CoAs must adhere to all general laboratory safety procedures, including:
*   Wearing appropriate Personal Protective Equipment (PPE) such as lab coats, safety glasses, and gloves.
*   Proper handling and disposal of chemical reagents and samples.
*   Following all safety data sheet (SDS) instructions for hazardous materials.
*   Ensuring proper ventilation in laboratory areas.
*   Reporting any safety incidents or near-misses immediately.

## 8. Regulatory Compliance References

This SOP is developed in accordance with, but not limited to, the following regulatory guidelines and standards:
*   **U.S. Food and Drug Administration (FDA):** 21 CFR Part 211 (Current Good Manufacturing Practice for Finished Pharmaceuticals), 21 CFR Part 210 (Current Good Manufacturing Practice in Manufacturing, Processing, Packing, or Holding of Drugs).
*   **U.S. Department of Agriculture (USDA):** Relevant regulations pertaining to veterinary biologics and animal health products, where applicable.
*   **Current Good Manufacturing Practices (cGMP):** General principles of cGMP as applied to pharmaceutical manufacturing and quality control operations.
*   **International Council for Harmonisation (ICH) Guidelines:** Specifically, ICH Q7 (Good Manufacturing Practice Guide for Active Pharmaceutical Ingredients) and ICH Q10 (Pharmaceutical Quality System), which provide guidance on quality systems and manufacturing practices.
