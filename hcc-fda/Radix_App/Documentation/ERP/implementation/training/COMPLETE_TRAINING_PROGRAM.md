## 📚 TRAINING PROGRAM OVERVIEW

### Mission Statement
Develop and maintain a comprehensive GMP training program that ensures all personnel are qualified, competent, and current in their assigned duties to produce safe, effective veterinary pharmaceutical products in compliance with all applicable regulations.

### Program Objectives
1. **Ensure Compliance:** 100% regulatory compliance with FDA, USDA, and other requirements
2. **Build Competency:** Verify all personnel competent in assigned duties
3. **Maintain Currency:** Keep all training current and relevant
4. **Document Thoroughly:** Complete, accurate training records
5. **Continuous Improvement:** Regular program assessment and enhancement

### Regulatory Basis
- **FDA 21 CFR Part 211.25:** Personnel qualifications
- **FDA 21 CFR Part 211.28:** Personnel responsibilities
- **FDA 21 CFR Part 110:** Current Good Manufacturing Practice
- **ICH Q10:** Pharmaceutical Quality System
- **ISO 9001:2015:** Quality Management Systems

## 🎓 TRAINING CURRICULUM STRUCTURE

### Level 1: Foundation Training (Required for ALL Personnel)

#### Module 1.1: GMP Fundamentals (8 hours)
**Learning Objectives:**
- Define GMP and explain its importance
- Identify key regulatory requirements
- Describe quality culture principles
- Explain personal responsibilities

**Content Outline:**
1. **Introduction to GMP (1 hour)**
   - What is GMP?
   - History and evolution
   - Regulatory landscape (FDA, USDA, ICH)
   - Why GMP matters

2. **Quality Culture & Mindset (1.5 hours)**
   - What is quality culture?
   - Everyone's responsibility
   - Speaking up and stop authority
   - Ethical conduct

3. **Documentation Fundamentals (2 hours)**
   - Importance of documentation
   - "If it isn't documented, it didn't happen"
   - Data integrity (ALCOA+)
   - Good documentation practices
   - Common documentation errors

4. **Personal Hygiene & GMP Practices (1.5 hours)**
   - Personal cleanliness requirements
   - Hand washing procedures
   - Jewelry and cosmetics restrictions
   - Eating, drinking, smoking prohibitions
   - Illness and injury reporting

5. **Contamination Control (1.5 hours)**
   - Types of contamination
   - Cross-contamination prevention
   - Gowning and PPE
   - Cleaning and sanitization
   - Environmental controls

6. **Safety & Security (0.5 hours)**
   - Basic safety rules
   - Emergency procedures
   - Security requirements
   - Reporting concerns

**Delivery Method:** Instructor-led classroom training  
**Duration:** Full day (8 hours)  
**Assessment:** Written test (80% passing score)  
**Frequency:** Initial hire, annual refresher (4 hours)

**Materials Provided:**
- Student workbook
- Quick reference guide
- GMP pocket guide
- Certificate of completion

## 📝 ASSESSMENT & COMPETENCY VERIFICATION

### Assessment Methods

1. **Written Tests**
   - Multiple choice (20-30 questions)
   - True/False (10-15 questions)
   - Short answer (5-10 questions)
   - Passing score: 80%
   - Two attempts allowed

2. **Practical Demonstrations**
   - Observed performance of skills
   - Competency checklist completion
   - Pass/Fail evaluation
   - Remediation if needed

3. **Case Studies**
   - Real-world scenario analysis
   - Written response required
   - Evaluated on comprehension and application
   - Graded rubric

4. **On-the-Job Training (OJT)**
   - Supervised work performance
   - Competency checklist (10-20 items per role)
   - Progressive responsibility
   - Final sign-off by trainer and supervisor

### Competency Verification Process

1. **Initial Training:** Complete all required modules
2. **Assessment:** Pass written tests and practical exams
3. **OJT:** Supervised performance (2-4 weeks)
4. **Competency Check:** Final verification by supervisor
5. **Certification:** Training certificate issued
6. **Ongoing:** Annual refresher training required
