# Standard Operating Procedure: Raw Material Handling and Storage for Pharmaceutical Manufacturing

**SOP Number:** SOP-MFG-002

**SOP Title:** Raw Material Handling and Storage for Pharmaceutical Manufacturing

**Category:** Manufacturing

**Compliance Areas:** FDA, USDA, cGMP

**Version:** 1.0

**Effective Date:** 2025-08-12

## 1. Purpose and Scope

This Standard Operating Procedure (SOP) outlines the procedures for the proper handling, storage, and control of raw materials used in large animal pharmaceutical manufacturing operations. The purpose of this SOP is to ensure the quality, integrity, and traceability of all raw materials from receipt to their use in production, in compliance with current Good Manufacturing Practices (cGMP) and relevant regulatory requirements, including those from the U.S. Food and Drug Administration (FDA) and the U.S. Department of Agriculture (USDA).

This SOP applies to all personnel involved in the receiving, inspection, sampling, quarantine, storage, and dispensing of raw materials within the manufacturing facility.

## 2. Responsibilities

**2.1. Quality Assurance (QA) Department:**
*   Approving or rejecting raw materials based on quality control testing.
*   Ensuring compliance with cGMP regulations and internal quality standards.
*   Reviewing and approving this SOP and any subsequent revisions.
*   Conducting audits of raw material handling and storage practices.

**2.2. Warehouse/Receiving Personnel:**
*   Receiving and inspecting incoming raw materials.
*   Ensuring proper labeling and segregation of materials.
*   Placing materials in designated quarantine areas.
*   Maintaining cleanliness and organization of storage areas.
*   Notifying Quality Control (QC) for sampling.

**2.3. Quality Control (QC) Department:**
*   Collecting samples of raw materials for testing.
*   Performing analytical and microbiological testing of raw materials.
*   Releasing or rejecting raw materials based on test results.
*   Maintaining records of all testing performed.

**2.4. Production Department:**
*   Issuing requisitions for raw materials.
*   Ensuring proper handling of materials during dispensing and transfer to manufacturing.
*   Reporting any discrepancies or damage to raw materials.

**2.5. All Personnel:**
*   Adhering to the procedures outlined in this SOP.
*   Reporting any deviations or incidents related to raw material handling.
*   Participating in required training programs.

## 3. Detailed Procedures

### 3.1. Receipt of Raw Materials

3.1.1. **Verification of Shipment:** Upon arrival, receiving personnel shall verify that the raw material shipment matches the purchase order and delivery documents (e.g., packing list, Certificate of Analysis (CoA)). This includes checking:
*   Supplier name and address.
*   Product name and code.
*   Quantity received.
*   Batch number/Lot number.
*   Manufacturing and expiry dates.
*   Condition of containers and seals (e.g., no damage, leaks, or signs of tampering).

3.1.2. **Documentation:** All incoming shipments shall be recorded in the Raw Material Receipt Log. The log shall include:
*   Date of receipt.
*   Supplier name.
*   Product name and code.
*   Batch/Lot number.
*   Quantity received.
*   Purchase order number.
*   Receiving personnel's signature.
*   Any observed discrepancies or damage.

3.1.3. **Quarantine:** All received raw materials shall be immediately transferred to a designated 


quarantine area. The quarantine area must be clearly demarcated and labeled to prevent accidental use of unapproved materials. Each container shall be labeled with a "Quarantine" status label, indicating:
*   Product name.
*   Batch/Lot number.
*   Date of receipt.
*   Quarantine status.

### 3.2. Sampling of Raw Materials

3.2.1. **Notification to QC:** Receiving personnel shall notify the QC Department immediately upon placing raw materials in quarantine to initiate the sampling process.

3.2.2. **Sampling Procedure:** QC personnel shall collect samples of raw materials according to established sampling plans and procedures. Sampling shall be performed in a controlled environment (e.g., sampling booth) to prevent contamination and ensure sample integrity. All sampling tools and equipment must be clean and sterilized.

3.2.3. **Sample Labeling:** Each sample container shall be clearly labeled with:
*   Product name.
*   Batch/Lot number.
*   Date of sampling.
*   Sampler's initials.
*   Unique sample identification number.

3.2.4. **Return to Quarantine:** After sampling, the raw material containers shall be re-sealed and returned to the quarantine area, maintaining their "Quarantine" status label.

### 3.3. Testing and Release of Raw Materials

3.3.1. **Laboratory Testing:** QC personnel shall perform all required analytical, physical, and microbiological tests on the collected samples as per approved specifications. All test results shall be accurately recorded.

3.3.2. **Evaluation of Results:** The QC Department shall evaluate the test results against the established raw material specifications. Any out-of-specification (OOS) results or deviations shall be investigated thoroughly according to the OOS procedure.

3.3.3. **Release or Rejection:**
*   **Released Materials:** If all tests meet the specifications, the QC Department shall change the status of the raw material from "Quarantine" to "Released" or "Approved." A "Released" status label shall be affixed to each container, and the materials shall be moved to the designated approved storage area.
*   **Rejected Materials:** If any test fails to meet specifications, or if there are unresolved OOS results or deviations, the raw material shall be labeled "Rejected" and moved to a designated rejected materials area. Rejected materials shall be handled and disposed of according to the Rejected Materials SOP.

### 3.4. Storage of Raw Materials

3.4.1. **Designated Storage Areas:** Raw materials shall be stored in designated areas that are clean, dry, well-ventilated, and protected from extreme temperatures, humidity, light, and pests. Different types of materials (e.g., active pharmaceutical ingredients (APIs), excipients, packaging materials) shall be segregated to prevent cross-contamination.

3.4.2. **Environmental Control:** Storage areas shall be monitored for temperature and humidity, and records maintained. Specific storage conditions (e.g., refrigeration, freezing) required for certain raw materials shall be strictly adhered to.

3.4.3. **First-In, First-Out (FIFO) / First-Expiring, First-Out (FEFO):** Raw materials shall be stored and rotated to ensure that the oldest approved materials or those with the earliest expiration dates are used first. This helps minimize waste and ensures material potency.

3.4.4. **Labeling and Identification:** All stored raw materials shall be clearly labeled with:
*   Product name.
*   Batch/Lot number.
*   Manufacturing and expiration dates.
*   Current status (e.g., Released, On-Hold).
*   Storage conditions.

3.4.5. **Security:** Access to raw material storage areas shall be restricted to authorized personnel only. Security measures shall be in place to prevent theft, damage, or unauthorized access.

### 3.5. Dispensing of Raw Materials

3.5.1. **Requisition:** Production personnel shall submit a formal requisition for raw materials required for a specific batch. The requisition shall include:
*   Product name.
*   Batch/Lot number.
*   Quantity required.
*   Manufacturing order number.

3.5.2. **Dispensing Procedure:** Dispensing of raw materials shall be performed by trained personnel in a controlled dispensing area to prevent contamination and ensure accurate weighing/measuring. Each dispensed quantity shall be verified by a second person.

3.5.3. **Labeling of Dispensed Materials:** Dispensed materials shall be immediately labeled with:
*   Product name.
*   Batch/Lot number.
*   Quantity dispensed.
*   Date of dispensing.
*   Manufacturing order number.
*   Dispensing personnel's initials.

3.5.4. **Return of Unused Materials:** Any unused dispensed raw materials shall be returned to the approved storage area and re-entered into inventory, provided their integrity and quality have not been compromised. This process shall be documented.

## 4. Documentation Requirements

All activities related to raw material handling and storage shall be thoroughly documented. This includes, but is not limited to:
*   Raw Material Receipt Log.
*   Sampling records.
*   Laboratory testing records (including CoAs).
*   Raw material release/rejection records.
*   Temperature and humidity monitoring logs for storage areas.
*   Dispensing records.
*   Inventory records.
*   Deviation reports and investigation records.
*   Training records.

All documentation shall be accurate, legible, and maintained in a secure and retrievable manner for the required retention period as per regulatory guidelines.

## 5. Training Requirements

All personnel involved in raw material handling and storage shall receive initial and ongoing training on this SOP, relevant cGMP regulations, safety procedures, and specific job functions. Training shall be documented and include:
*   SOP review and understanding.
*   Practical demonstration of procedures.
*   Assessment of competency.
*   Refresher training at regular intervals or when procedures change.

## 6. Quality Control Measures

*   **Supplier Qualification:** Raw materials shall only be procured from qualified and approved suppliers. Supplier qualification shall include assessment of their quality systems, manufacturing processes, and ability to consistently provide materials meeting specifications.
*   **Incoming Material Inspection:** 100% visual inspection of incoming raw material containers for damage, integrity, and proper labeling.
*   **Identity Testing:** Each batch of raw material shall undergo identity testing to confirm its authenticity.
*   **Full Compendial Testing:** Raw materials shall be tested according to pharmacopeial monographs (e.g., USP, EP, BP) or approved in-house specifications.
*   **Retest/Expiration Dating:** A robust program shall be in place for assigning and managing retest dates or expiration dates for raw materials, with appropriate retesting performed as needed.
*   **Stability Monitoring:** For critical raw materials, stability studies may be conducted to confirm their stability under defined storage conditions over time.

## 7. Safety Considerations

*   **Material Safety Data Sheets (MSDS)/Safety Data Sheets (SDS):** MSDS/SDS shall be readily available for all raw materials and personnel shall be trained on the hazards and safe handling procedures for each material.
*   **Personal Protective Equipment (PPE):** Appropriate PPE (e.g., gloves, safety glasses, respirators, lab coats) shall be worn by personnel during handling, sampling, and dispensing activities as required by the MSDS/SDS and risk assessment.
*   **Spill Procedures:** Procedures for handling spills of raw materials shall be established, and spill kits shall be readily available. Personnel shall be trained on spill containment and cleanup.
*   **Emergency Procedures:** Emergency procedures, including first aid, fire safety, and evacuation plans, shall be in place and communicated to all personnel.
*   **Ventilation:** Adequate ventilation shall be provided in receiving, sampling, storage, and dispensing areas to minimize exposure to airborne particles or fumes.

## 8. Regulatory Compliance References

This SOP is developed in accordance with, but not limited to, the following regulatory guidelines:

*   **U.S. Food and Drug Administration (FDA):**
    *   21 CFR Part 211 - Current Good Manufacturing Practice for Finished Pharmaceuticals (specifically Subpart E - Control of Components and Drug Product Containers and Closures, and Subpart H - Laboratory Controls).
    *   Guidance for Industry: Q7A Good Manufacturing Practice Guidance for Active Pharmaceutical Ingredients.
    *   FDA regulations pertaining to animal drugs (e.g., 21 CFR Part 510 - New Animal Drugs).

*   **U.S. Department of Agriculture (USDA):**
    *   USDA regulations related to veterinary biologics and animal products, particularly for large animal pharmaceutical manufacturing where applicable (e.g., 9 CFR - Animals and Animal Products).

*   **Current Good Manufacturing Practices (cGMP):**
    *   General cGMP principles as applied to pharmaceutical manufacturing, ensuring quality systems are in place for raw material control.

## 9. Change Control Procedures

Any changes to this SOP, raw material specifications, handling procedures, storage conditions, or equipment used shall be managed through a formal change control system. All changes shall be documented, reviewed, and approved by relevant departments (e.g., QA, QC, Manufacturing) before implementation. Training on revised procedures shall be conducted and documented.
